# How to Test: Computer Vision Features

Run `python raw_view.py` and use the following keys:

1. Face Detection:
   - Press `f`.
   - Look at the camera; verify a green box appears around your face with a "Face" label.

2. QR Code Reading:
   - Press `z`.
   - Hold a QR code in front of the camera.
   - Verify the decoded text appears at the bottom of the screen.

3. Motion Detection:
   - Press `m`.
   - Move an object in the frame.
   - Verify red bounding boxes appear around moving areas and "MOTION" text appears.

4. Telemetry Overlay:
   - Press `t`.
   - Verify the cyan text overlay showing Heap, RSSI, Temp, and PSRAM appears on the left.
